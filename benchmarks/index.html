    Benchmarks on public tabular data¶

    PhasmaNet was developed for a scientific surrogate task (the optimal SNR of gravitational-wave signals), but the architecture itself is general. The appendix of the paper tests it on three standard public tabular benchmarks: one regression and two classification problems. It is compared with the nine deep tabular architectures reported by Gorishniy et al. (2021), on the same train/validation/test splits.

    How to read these numbers

    PhasmaNet was run out of the box: the configuration, tokenizer and training protocol are the ones of the SNR task, with no tuning on these datasets. The reference architectures were each tuned per dataset with Bayesian optimisation. The results show that the architecture transfers to other data; they are not a claim of state-of-the-art performance.

    Dataset Task Features Metric PhasmaNet (untuned) Rank among 10 deep models Best tuned reference
    Covertype multiclass classification (7 classes) 54 accuracy ↑ 0.9664 ± 0.0002 2 / 9¹ FT-Transformer 0.970
    YearPredictionMSD regression 90 RMSE ↓ 8.8738 6 / 10 NODE 8.784
    Epsilon binary classification 2,000 accuracy ↑ 0.8915 ± 0.0005 9 / 10 FT-Transformer 0.8982

    ¹ GrowNet reports no Covertype result, so nine models are ranked.

    Covertype¶

    PhasmaNet reaches an accuracy of 0.9664 and outperforms seven of the eight reference architectures: DCN2 (0.965), ResNet (0.964), MLP (0.962), SNN (0.961), NODE (0.958), TabNet (0.957) and AutoInt (0.934). Only FT-Transformer (0.970) is higher.

    PhasmaNet variant Accuracy Cross-entropy
    Φ-Refractor ON (default) 0.9664 ± 0.0002 0.2828 ± 0.0049
    Φ-Refractor OFF 0.9634

    YearPredictionMSD¶

    PhasmaNet reaches an RMSE of 8.8738. It outperforms TabNet (8.909), SNN (8.895), DCN2 (8.890) and AutoInt (8.882), and is within 0.09 RMSE of the best reference, NODE (8.784).

    Epsilon¶

    Epsilon has 2,000 dense input features, two orders of magnitude more than the task the PhasmaNet tokenizer was sized for, so it is a deliberate stress test. PhasmaNet reaches an accuracy of 0.8915 ± 0.0005, a ROC-AUC of 0.9950 ± 0.0003 and a scaled log loss (×100) of 25.97 ± 0.13. All ten deep models lie within about 0.9 percentage points of accuracy of each other here. PhasmaNet is 0.67 points below the best and above TabNet (0.8896).
